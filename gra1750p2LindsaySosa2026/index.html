Classic Chocolate Éclairs

Classic Chocolate Éclairs are a traditional French pastry made with light and airy choux dough, filled with smooth vanilla pastry cream, and finished with a rich chocolate glaze. They are elegant, creamy, and perfect for a French-inspired dessert.

Ingredients

For the Choux Pastry

  • ½ cup water
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Remove from heat and add the flour all at once. Stir until a smooth dough forms.
  3. Let the dough cool slightly. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  4. Transfer the dough to a piping bag and pipe long strips onto the baking sheet. Bake until the éclairs are puffed and golden.
  5. Prepare the pastry cream by heating the milk. In a bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, sugar, and cornstarch. Slowly add the warm milk and cook until thickened. Stir in the vanilla.
  6. Allow the éclairs and pastry cream to cool. Fill each éclair with the vanilla pastry cream.
  7. Dip the tops of the éclairs into the chocolate glaze or spread the glaze over them.
  8. Chill briefly, then serve and enjoy!
